## Summary of gaps → what SmartTicket addresses

1. **Scalping and bulk buying** (sources 1, 2): a trigger enforces a per-customer limit of 6 tickets per event.
2. **Fake or duplicate tickets** (sources 3, 5): unique seat-per-event constraints and a trigger that blocks double-selling a seat.
3. **Re-entry and ticket sharing at the gate** (source 3): a gate-scan trigger rejects a second scan of a ticket that has already been used.
4. **No transparency or reporting** (sources 4, 5): an audit log plus a live Power BI dashboard (source 8) showing sales, occupancy and denied attempts.
